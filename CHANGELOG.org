#+TITLE: Changelog

* Unreleased
- Time data only: a measurement with the hammer as the only enabled channel (no responses) records
  and saves the hits as time data (TDMS, optional UFF 58 time records, hit and trigger logs) with
  the usual trigger, checks and external trigger output; no FRFs are computed or saved. The
  decay check is skipped without responses.

* 0.1.0b1 — first beta (November 2026)
First public release of pymodalhammer under the GNU GPL v3 or later.
- Channel setup, acquisition setup (record, trigger, windows, averaging, hit checks), probe mode,
  measurement with hit review, roving-hammer points, Data Viewer with re-processing and comparison.
- External trigger output: hardware-timed (own clock + AI start trigger, or AI sample clock with
  automatic fallback) or on demand; frame sync; pulses shown in probe mode; trigger log.
- UFF 58 (FRF, coherence, auto-spectra, time) and TDMS (hits, continuous record) export.
- Simulation back end, mock-driver tests and GUI workflow tests.
- User manual (Org, exportable to PDF) with screenshots.
